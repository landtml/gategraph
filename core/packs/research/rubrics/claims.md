# Rubric: claims and sources (verifier, research pack)

The checks already decide whether each claim cites a source and whether its
number appears in that source. A verifier judges what the checks cannot.

For each claim line in the report, answer pass or fail:

1. Wording: the sentence around the claim says what the cited source says,
   with no stronger conclusion than the source supports.
2. Scope: the claim's period, population and unit match the source's.
3. Omission: the report does not present a claim as settled when the source
   is silent or contradicts it.

Any fail is a fail of the gate. Name the claim line and the sentence that
overreaches. Do not judge the numbers: `claims-verified` does that.

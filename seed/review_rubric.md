# Review rubric

Criteria the blinded reviewer scores every artifact against. Replace these
seeds with project-specific criteria; keep each criterion checkable against
the artifact alone (the reviewer has no other context).

1. **Complete**: the artifact fulfils its stated purpose end-to-end; no
   placeholders, TODOs, or truncated sections.
2. **Internally consistent**: no claim in one part contradicted by another.
3. **Evidence-backed**: every factual claim is sourced or derivable from the
   artifact itself.
4. **Fit for the unit**: matches the unit's declared scope (its directory
   name / manifest), neither padded nor missing required parts.
